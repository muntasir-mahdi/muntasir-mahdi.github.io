This project focused on developing a **Nb and Cu coplanar waveguide (CPW)** platform with **indium bump electroplating** to enable **flip-chip bonding** with magnetic target samples for **magnon–magnon coupling** measurements.  
The fabrication work was completed up to the indium plating stage; the final flip-chip bonding was planned but not executed during the initial phase.

### Indium Electroplating for Flip-Chip Integration

The final step involved **indium bump electroplating** to form bonding pillars at the designed pad sites. These bumps were intended to interface with magnetic thin-film samples for hybrid magnonic coupling studies. The electroplating process was optimized for **uniformity, adhesion, and surface morphology**, using a pulsed DC current and temperature-controlled bath.

### Motivation and Future Work

This structure was designed to couple **superconducting microwave resonators** with **magnetic thin films** for quantum-hybrid systems. The next steps will involve:

- Completing flip-chip bonding using thermocompression.  
- Performing **S-parameter and low-temperature microwave measurements** to analyze **magnon–magnon coupling strength**.  
- Integrating **cryo-compatible packaging** for dilution refrigerator testing.
