This project involved the **design and assembly of a high-vacuum ion milling system** for advanced material processing and characterization.  
Key achievements included **integration of Ar ion gun, turbo and roughing pumps**, and the creation of **custom 3D-designed sample holders** using Adobe Fusion 360.

### Key Features & Techniques

- **High-Vacuum Ion Milling System:** Designed for precise material removal and surface modification.  
- **Ar Ion Gun Integration:** Achieved directional milling and surface etching control.  
- **Pump Configuration:** Turbo and roughing pumps for achieving high vacuum levels.  
- **Custom Sample Holders:** 3D-designed in Adobe Fusion 360 for precise alignment and secure mounting of samples.  
- **System Assembly:** Mechanical and electrical integration for full operational capability.
