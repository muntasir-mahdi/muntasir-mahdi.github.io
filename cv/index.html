Summary Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech specializing in semiconductor devices, epitaxial growth, and quantum photonic systems. My research explores the design, fabrication, and physical modeling of group-IV and 2D material systems, focusing on Ge/GeSn integration, carrier transport, and optoelectronic coupling at the nanoscale. I bridge experimental and computational approaches across thin film growth, device characterization, and TCAD modeling for next-generation nanoelectronics.
