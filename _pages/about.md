I am a **Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Virginia Tech](https://ece.vt.edu/)**, focusing on **semiconductor devices, fabrication, and circuit–device co-design**.  
My work bridges **materials and device physics** with **integrated circuit and photonic applications**.

Before joining Virginia Tech, I completed my **M.S. at [Auburn University](https://www.auburn.edu/)** and **B.S. at [Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST)](https://www.sust.edu/)**, Bangladesh.

### Research Overview

My research focuses on **semiconductor devices** for advanced electronic and photonic systems.  
I work on:

- **Epitaxial Germanium Films** — Characterizing MBE-grown Ge for CMOS-compatible integration.  
- **Superconducting Circuits** — Fabrication and measurement of Nb-based resonators and Josephson junctions.  
- **Device–Circuit Co-Design** — Linking fabrication results to circuit-level performance.

I use and develop tools for **device simulation (TCAD)**, **measurement automation (Python)**, and **cleanroom fabrication workflows**.
