### Short Bio

I am a Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech specializing in semiconductor devices and circuit–device co-design. My background includes over three years of experience in microfabrication, process integration, and system prototyping through federally funded research projects. I have authored 8 journal papers and 3 conference papers. My work involves rapid design–test iterations, cross-disciplinary engineering, and Python programming for automation and data analysis.

### Research Interests

- Semiconductor device physics and integrated photonics
- TCAD modeling and circuit-device co-design
- Microfabrication, thin-film epitaxy, and superconducting devices
- Measurement techniques including low-temperature, RF, and optical characterization
- System prototyping and hardware-software integration

### Skills

**Fabrication & Packaging:** Photolithography, E-beam lithography, Liftoff, Electroplating, DC/RF Sputtering, PECVD, ICP Etching, Ion Milling, Wedge Bonding, Soldering  
**Characterization:** Ellipsometry, Oscilloscope, QD-PPMS Cryostat, AFM, XRD, VNA, SEM  
**Simulation & Design:** Keysight ADS, Cadence Virtuoso, Altium Designer, Autodesk Fusion 360, Sentaurus TCAD (learning)  
**Programming & Automation:** Python, C, MATLAB, OriginLab, DAQ systems, Microcontroller, Raspberry Pi, Linux scripting

### Technical Experience & Projects

**Graduate Research Assistant, Virginia Tech (Aug 2024 – Present)**

- Characterized MBE-grown Ge epi-films, analyzing defects, strain, and critical-point transitions using ellipsometry
- Validated film quality against CMOS process requirements and correlated defects with device performance

**Graduate Research Assistant, Auburn University (May 2021 – Aug 2024)**

- Designed and fabricated high-Q superconducting resonators, coplanar waveguides, and Nb-based Josephson junctions
- Performed low-temperature measurements using QD-PPMS, VNA, AFM/MFM, XRD
- Developed Python automation for transport and resonance measurements, reducing testing costs
- Built custom high-vacuum ion milling system and designed cryogenic PCB/sample holders (Fusion 360/Altium)

**Undergraduate Research, SUST, Bangladesh (2015 – 2019)**

- CNTFET modeling and simulation using MATLAB/Simulink
- Mars Rover robot project: motor control, mechanical integration, competed internationally
